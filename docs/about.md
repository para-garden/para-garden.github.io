# About para

para-garden tends worlds — concrete games, experiences, and creative works. Things people actually encounter and inhabit.

Where [rhi](https://docs.rhi.zone/about) is infrastructure and [exo](https://docs.exo.place/about) is places/platforms, para is the things that grow in them.

## Philosophy

A garden is never finished. You tend it — you plant things, see what grows, pull weeds, make space. The things in it are alive in the sense that they change, respond, surprise you.

para projects are like that:

- **Tended, not finished.** Nothing ships as a final product. Things grow, change, get reworked.
- **Constrained, not limited.** Intentional boundaries create focus. A text game doesn't need graphics — it needs good writing and honest mechanics.
- **Inhabited, not consumed.** These are things you spend time in, not things you scroll past.

## Relationship to rhi and exo

Three organizations, three concerns:

- **[rhi](https://docs.rhi.zone)** — infrastructure. The substrate: tooling, libraries, protocols.
- **[exo](https://docs.exo.place)** — places. Biomes and platforms where things can happen.
- **para** — things. Concrete works that people encounter.

para projects may use rhi infrastructure and live in exo places, but they don't depend on either. They're the fruit, not the tree.
